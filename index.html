    What is acrylamide?
    Acrylamide forms in starchy foods when baked above ~120°C (Maillard reaction). EU Regulation 2017/2158 requires bakeries to monitor crust colour as a mitigation measure. Darker crust = higher acrylamide. The Flourish shade cards (scales 1–4) are the official colour reference for this product range.
